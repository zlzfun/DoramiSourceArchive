## Article analysis and governed taxonomy (v3.44)

Article persistence is the reliability boundary; analysis is queued afterward and processed by restart-safe leases in `services/article_analysis.py`. A succeeded current asset contains the news-value score (column `quality_score`, semantics since v3.48 = 这件事有多重要, one ruler for reader/personal brief/public brief)/summary/genre plus ordered canonical assignments and up to six extracted display tags. Canonical recall uses names, aliases and `prompt_description`; extracted tags may remain flexible display/search chips without becoming long-term interests. `services/taxonomy.py` owns global taxonomy versions, aliases, parent relations, Candidate evidence/decisions, publication and retag jobs. The approved v1 catalog is repository-controlled; neither bootstrap nor automatic activation may silently publish product vocabulary.

## Personal brief selection (v3.44)

User interests are `follow` only since v3.56 (issue #27 retired the `mute` stance; the column stays for compatibility); all three canonical facets are initially selectable, while taxonomy policy may later cap hot choices per facet. A personal edition freezes the user's subscribed source IDs and interest revision. The value baseline ranks all subscribed candidates, including interest matches; interest promotions may replace only its tail, at most 30% of actual curated output (2026-11-24 decision; default target 10 means at most 3). Baseline matches keep the quality lane and their topic labels without consuming promotion slots (hard floor 5.0 since v3.48), and an empty subscription scope stays empty. Edition items are immutable snapshots. Since v3.48 editions generate immediately from available content (去等待): `sync_stale`/`analysis_incomplete` are recorded as facts at generation time, not waited on; since issue #343 they also mark an edition the system may still replace when new content lands (see the rebuild paragraph below). The public daily brief remains a separate product that reuses the same analysis score. **v3.50 adds a third lane `breaking`(「重大事件」,issue #33 §2)** that deliberately ignores the subscription scope: an event qualifies mechanically when an official source scores ≥ T(default 9.0)or ≥2 distinct sources share an `entity.*` tag with scores ≥ T−0.5 and one ≥ T; events are merged by entity-code connected components, one representative each(official > non-social > score > earlier), at most N(default 2,0 = off)per edition, published within 24h, hidden/`user_rss_` sources excluded, entities featured in the reader's previous two dates suppressed. Breaking items sit at positions 0..N−1 in section「重大事件」**on top of** the target 10 and never count toward the interest ratio; an empty subscription scope still yields no edition. **v3.52.1(issue #33 §4)** localizes item titles after generation: `personal_digest_titles.localize_edition_titles` fills `snapshot.title_zh` from the article's `translation_zh_title` cache, then recent public-brief `title_cn`, then a budgeted aux-model batch translation written back to the cache; Chinese titles are skipped, failures fall back to the original title, the edition never fails because of it. Knobs: KV `personal_digest_breaking_min_score`/`personal_digest_breaking_max_items` via `/api/admin/analysis/config`. See `docs/archive/personal-brief-breaking-lane-plan.md`. **v3.54.0(issue #33 §3)「订阅 ∪ 兴趣」**: the interest lane is no longer cut by the subscription boundary——its pool is 订阅源 ∪(全站可见源 ∩ 命中兴趣标签)[v3.56 起无屏蔽]; outside-subscription candidates(`DigestArticleCandidateDTO.subscribed=False`)enter **only** the interest lane, must clear `personal_digest_external_min_score`(default 6.0,订阅内仍 5.0)and share a hard `personal_digest_external_per_source_max`(default 2,never relaxed); subscribed hits rank first; the quality lane stays subscription-only; an interest hit(both pools)means primary tag or relevance ≥ 0.8(same ruler as the reader interest lens); an empty subscription **with** followed interests now yields an interest-only edition(≤ 3 items under the 2026-11-24 default allocation, ratio lifted), only 订阅+兴趣 both empty stays `empty_subscriptions`; freeze/readiness(`expected_source_ids`/`due`/`sync_stale`)remain subscription-only; feed/MCP unchanged. Editions persist `selection_stats_json`(candidate counts/window/followed/hits/breaking)for the page's「编排说明行」. See `docs/archive/personal-brief-interest-union.md`. **事件层 wave 2(issue #103)**: same-event dedup consumes the platform event layer (`DigestArticleCandidateDTO.event_id`, `digest_selection.event_key` = `event:<id>` → canonical URL → normalised title): the key is shared across the breaking / interest / quality lanes (an event already headlined never reappears in the curated lanes; inside the curated lanes same-event candidates collapse to one representative chosen by official > non-social > score > earlier **before** lane ranking), any event with a member in the reader's previous two editions is suppressed as a whole today (degraded fallback included; the breaking lane only honours events that *headlined* in those two editions, so an escalation inside a previously-minor event may still headline once), item snapshots carry `event_id` + `related_sources`(the event's other reader-visible members via `event_board.visible_member_links`, rendered as「另见 N 篇」), and `selection_stats_json.same_event_merged` / `same_event_previous_day` feed the fact line. Per-reader generation still makes zero LLM calls. `POLICY_VERSION` is `personal-digest-v3`; earlier editions retain their frozen policy and lane.

## Daily Brief (每日 AI 资讯日报) — reuse the article analysis score, then deterministic render

发布事务会在生成节点追加一份跨天判定快照，保存全部预选正文的编辑结果、实际输入、原始返回及最终去留。管理员可以按日期和批次复核，详情见 [#313 取证与验收协议](../plans/issue-313-cross-day-dedup.md)。

`src/services/daily_brief.py` orchestrates a scheduled/manual digest of already-archived articles. Since v3.48 (issue #22) the brief **does not score articles itself**: the platform has one scoring ruler — the article-analysis news-value prompt (`llm/article_analysis_prompt.py`) — and the brief consumes it. Pipeline: `collect_candidates` (cursor/allowlist/batch caps; overflow persists as pending in `daily_brief_candidates`; default batches reserve up to 40 backlog slots then admit new items within 120 total / 60 per source) → `load_stored_scores` (batch-read `article_analyses` rows that are `succeeded` **and** carry the current `scoring_version`; older rulers count as absent) → `score_candidates` (stored scores project directly; candidates the worker has not reached are scored **in place by the same `analyze_article_with_llm`** fed the same `load_relevant_active_tags` closed set the worker uses (so realm/company/tags share one vocabulary), concurrent + one serial retry; body-less candidates are title-scored by the same ruler; results are used for this run only and **never written back** — the brief and the analysis worker stay decoupled, so the brief works with the analysis switch off or with zero analysed rows, worst-case cost = the old map) → threshold (`daily_brief_min_score` KV, default 6.0, 0 = off: below-threshold items are passed — neither selected nor appended, skipped by the cursor; body-less items that clear it go to the appendix) → `dedup_clusters` (`precluster_by_event` first folds members of the same platform event [issue #103, `event_members` current membership, zero LLM], then `precluster_same_event` merges same-company titles that are near-identical after normalisation with matching digit runs — mechanical, LLM-free; then one LLM call clusters the rest; official representative preferred within a 1.0 gap, media links folded into `extra_sources`; LLM failure keeps the mechanical merge) → `select_top` (effective score = score + 0.5×official bounded bonus [v3.35], per-source/per-realm caps and a `paper_cap`; classification/realm/company are derived mechanically from the analysis genre / first topic tag / first entity; preselects top_n+buffer) → `editorial_polish` (**only the preselected dozen** get one LLM call each for title_cn / 1–3 bold key points / 100–150 字点评 / source name / company / realm / tags via `EDITORIAL_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, the retired MAP contract minus score and classification; the analysis summary and `score_reason` are passed in as known facts so the 6000-char body clip never loses the global picture; failure keeps the analysis summary as key points and an empty comment, never demotes to the appendix) → `cross_day_dedup` (small aux-tier JSON call against recent briefs' titles + first key point [excluding the report date]; official items never silently dropped — downgraded to a「（接前报）」followup) → trim to top_n → **same-day merge** (v3.35: rerun merges old∪new instead of overwriting) → `render_brief_markdown` (deterministic code-side rendering, v3.34) → idempotent write of a `daily_brief` content record (`source_id=dorami_daily_brief`). Items whose score never arrived (inline scoring failed after retry) and body-less items that cleared the threshold land in the「📎 其它收录」appendix as title+link — the appendix is no longer an ungated back door. `extensions.items` keeps its historical shape (title_cn/summary/comment/classification/score/tags/company/realm…; `score_reason` is never projected, so it can never be mistaken for the public comment) — the shendeng export and intranet pipeline are unaffected. **Three-layer dedup**: ① the deterministic high-water cursor `daily_brief_cursor` (over `fetched_date`) that only advances after a successful write; ② same-day `dedup_clusters`; ③ `cross_day_dedup`. Brief content, candidate consumption, cursor and last-run statistics commit atomically; generation has a per-database process lock and rejects concurrent scope/cursor changes. A changed explicit cursor resets the ledger for intentional replay; deleting the latest brief preserves old pending rows. Empty-cursor bootstrap is limited to the latest batch, including a fence for historical IDs sharing the watermark timestamp. Run state/config (cron, top-N, min-score, last run [incl. `scored_stored`/`scored_inline`/`scored_inline_pending`/`below_threshold`/`score_histogram`], cursor, **source-scope allowlist** `daily_brief_source_ids`) lives in `AppSettingRecord` KV; the node-local `daily_brief_candidates` ledger retains pending/processed article IDs (CASCADE on article deletion). `candidates_deferred` reports overflow. See `docs/archive/news-coverage-reliability-plan.md`. The allowlist is hand-maintained (unset = all sources). The scheduler registers exactly one APScheduler job `daily_brief`; `reload_daily_brief_schedule()` hot-adds/removes it. **Manual generation (`POST /api/daily-brief/generate`) is a persistent background job** returning `{status: "accepted", job_id}`; the frontend polls `GET /api/jobs/{job_id}`; fine-grained stage progress (collecting/scoring/selecting/editing/reducing/persisting) remains in-memory via `GET /api/daily-brief/progress`. Daily-brief endpoints are **collector(admin)-gated**; the panel (`DailyBriefPanel`) lives in the admin-only「AI 日报」tab.

推荐分发补充（#178，2026-11-11）：新闻与内容推荐分别选篇。个人早报及榜单「播客精选」只接受全文终评；公共日报另从近7天现有分析中挑选深读 / 播客，独立于新闻游标，不触发付费生成。新增推荐保存在 `extensions.content_recommendations` 并渲染到正文，旧 `items` / `included_article_ids` / `articles_count` 继续表示新闻、既有论文与附录；不由推荐提升新闻热度。默认名额、去重、混版本及历史边界见[方案 §6](../plans/issue-178-content-classification.md)。

## 生成流程(issue #72):公共日报是多个流程之一

平台自己定时把站内已有的内容加工成一份新内容,叫**生成流程**。现在有两个:公共日报、GitHub Trending 精读。`services/generation_flows.py` 只统一外面一层——登记、开关、cron、手动触发、进度、上次结果、往期;**内部各写各的**(`daily_brief.py` / `trending_picks.py`),不抽共用的管道:两份流程的取材、打分、选篇正是它们不一样的地方。加一个流程 = 写一个模块 + 在 `system.toml` 加一条系统源 + 在 `FLOWS` 里登记。

- **外壳认的东西**:一组有序的**阶段**(建议 ≤ 6;是内部过程的粗粒度投影,不是步骤图——日报的评分与点评是逐条并行、论文走单独名额,都在阶段里面)、阶段内进度(完成数 / 总数)、一次运行的结果(成功 / 跳过 / 失败;跳过不算失败)、配置表单的各行(由流程声明,管理面照着画)。并行或分支的画法等真有这样的流程再做,到时往阶段上加字段。
- **公共日报是薄适配**:配置键 `daily_brief_*`、调度里的 job id `daily_brief`、`/api/daily-brief/*` 都原样保留(神灯与 skill 模板在用);配置的校验与写入在 `daily_brief.apply_config_update`,日报自己的接口与外壳共用。
- **GitHub Trending 精读**:取最新一条 `github_trending_daily` 日榜——日榜的 id 与日期按 UTC 记,所以认的是「这条日榜有没有被写过」,不按日期对;辅助模型一次调用只返回结构化条目(榜单里没有的仓库、缺说明的、重复的都丢掉),正文由代码渲染;一天一期(北京时间的日期)。定时遇到已写过的榜单一律跳过;手动只在「写的就是今天这一期」时重写。失败不落库,原因记进上次结果。用量记在 `trending_picks`,不进读者预算。
- **产出是系统源的条目,不回流**:精读的源行 `analysis = false`(不评分、不打标),另有系统源名单的排除(见 [`fetchers-and-sources.md`](./fetchers-and-sources.md))与按条目类型的排除(`source_catalog.system_content_types()`:榜单、同源判重、候选治理)。读者面:别的系统源有了第一期才出现在目录里,日报照旧预先列出;个人早报不等日报以外的系统源。
- **调度**:`reload_generation_flow_schedule(flow_id)` 按流程 id 增删 cron job,一律经 `add_cron_job`;新流程缺省关。
- **管理面**:页签「生成流程」(id 仍是 `brief`),泳道一行一个流程,行上只有开关与立即生成;点一行打开抽屉(配置、往期)。样页与取舍见 [`design/issue-72-product-registry/`](../design/issue-72-product-registry/README.md)。
- **部署**:打开精读之前,接收节点要先升到含本功能的版本(旧版本会让订阅了精读的读者,个人早报每天等到截止);精读要 `github_trending_daily` 每天在采(它在观察期,不进每日自动采集)。详见[方案](../plans/issue-72-generation-flows.md) §7。

## Analysis visibility and worker draining (v3.46)

Missing historical analysis is absence, not score zero: reader surfaces omit score/tags until a result exists. Pending/running items show 「正在分析」; a forced same-content refresh keeps the prior result readable and shows 「更新中」 until an authority/content-hash-fenced commit replaces it. Changed content hides stale analysis. One scheduler cycle drains a bounded backlog with controlled concurrency instead of processing only one page, and pending/running metrics are reported separately.

## Personal brief rebuild intent (v3.46; entry points narrowed v3.51.1)

**Since issue #343 today's edition is replaced by the system, not by a reader action**:the reader page has no rebuild entry at all(页头「重新编排」、未就绪 / 偏好已更新 / 排队重编三种提示行、失败态「重试生成」都已取消;`POST /api/reader/briefs/today/rebuild` 保留给排查,前端不调用). The page polls today's payload while `refresh_pending` says so(`soon` every 3 s for the first minute, then 10 s — it is normally a matter of ~10 s but not guaranteed / `content` 60 s, `frontend/src/utils/briefRefresh.js`; overlapping requests are ordered so a late older response is dropped, and a change of the reader's subscription set while the page is open triggers one quiet fetch — `frontend/src/utils/briefSwap.js`); when a new revision arrives it swaps immediately if the reader is at the top of the page or has just returned to the tab, and holds it back while the reader is scrolled into the edition(released on scrolling back to the top)— no notice row, no action. While a published edition is about to be replaced(`refresh_pending == 'soon'`)the header's 编排时刻 slot reads「正在编排」(on the mobile shell, the date pill), and a system-initiated change of the page face is never a hard cut: the old face fades out(150 ms)and the new one fades in rising 12 px(`utils/spatialMotion.js` `leaveBrief` / `swapInBrief`, driven by `softSwap` in `PersonalBriefPage.jsx`; reduced motion = fade-in only). A failed edition that will still be retried is drawn as「编排中」. Three triggers run inside the 30 s `personal_digest_pending` job(`services/personal_digest_refresh.py`): ① **new content landed** — the node-local landing signal is the later of(a)the local completion time of the last Archive Sync `analyses` / `events` stream that actually inserted / updated / deleted rows, stamped by `remote_sync.record_v2_stream_success` into the sync state as `v2_content_landed_at`(same row and commit as the checkpoint, monotonic — a later no-change round cannot erase it; synced rows carry upstream timestamps and must never be compared with the local generation time)and(b)the last local analysis of a public source(KV `personal_digest:local_analysis_at`, stamped by the analysis job; a reader's custom sources do not count). After 3 quiet minutes(at most 15 when content keeps trickling in)and before 12:00(re-checked per account and again inside the write lock), every active account whose current edition still carries `sync_stale` / `analysis_incomplete` and predates the landing is re-selected, and a new revision(reason `content_arrived`)is published only when the layout(article + section order)differs; the processed landing is kept in KV `personal_digest:content_refresh` so later ticks short-circuit. Source readiness is deliberately **not** the trigger — `source_states` is the last sync stream, behind images and podcast audio. ② **interest / subscription edits** — the write paths(`PUT /api/reader/interests`; the subscription / custom-source writes matched by `_reader_subscription_write` in the auth middleware)park a latest-wins intent on today's current edition through `request_preference_rebuild`, only when `edition_freshness` says the frozen preferences really differ; the pass publishes once the intent has been quiet for 10 s and re-checks freshness first — and it does not wait for the 30 s tick: `note_preference_change` hands the due time(intent time + quiet period)to the scheduler(`announce_preference_intent` → `app.schedule_preference_refresh`, one date job per account, `replace_existing`), which runs `apply_preference_intents` right then; the 30 s job stays as the backstop, the hook is registered for the lifespan only, a due time is only ever moved later(interleaved requests may register out of order), and the pass is serialized by a process-level lock — the 30 s job waits for it, a due job does not(`wait=False`; when busy it schedules a single shared retry 2 s later instead of parking one thread per account)(edit-then-revert just clears the intent). Recording an intent is one write transaction(read current edition → compare → mark), in-flight editions only receive the mark, and a sweep every 10 minutes marks any current edition(ready / degraded / failed)that lags behind the reader's preferences without an intent — the backstop for a lost mark and for admin-side scope changes. Not bounded by the noon cut-off. When subscriptions and interests are both empty the pass publishes a **zero-item edition**(no lane contributes, not even breaking): the M2M contract projects it as `empty`, the reader today endpoints still answer `empty_subscriptions`(with a top-level `refresh_pending`), and it keeps carrying intents so re-subscribing recovers automatically. ③ **failure** — a failed current edition is retried 2 / 10 / 30 minutes after the last failure(reason `recovery`, at most three per day, re-validated under the write lock before the retry revision is created; the guest edition follows the same rule). ①② never create an in-flight revision: `publish_refresh` dry-runs the selection(`generate_personal_digest(dry_run=True)`), warms the title cache, then takes the write lock, verifies today's current edition is still the one it planned against, **recomputes scope and selection as of that moment**, and commits the terminal edition, its title-localized items(cache-only inside the lock), the frozen M2M projection and the supersession of earlier revisions in one transaction — neither readers nor the M2M contract(which returns the max revision of any status)observe an intermediate state, and a takedown or preference edit that happened during the title warm-up is never overwritten by stale input. **Every newly published same-day revision supersedes the earlier terminal ones in the same transaction**(`_supersede_predecessors`): one visible edition per day; superseded rows and their item snapshots are untouched and stay readable by date + revision. The today endpoints expose `refresh_pending`(`soon` — an intent is pending, the edition lags behind the current preferences, it is in flight, or a failure retry remains; `content` — flagged and before noon; `null`)so the page knows whether to come back, and still compute `interest_stale` / `scope_stale`(`personal_digest.edition_freshness`; the pass relies on it). This supersedes the v3.51.1 口径(edits recorded only, reader clicks「立即重编」— `docs/archive/personal-brief-rebuild-entrypoints.md`); plan: `docs/archive/issue-343-brief-auto-refresh.md`. Other system triggers are unchanged: the 08:30 schedule, the admin source-takedown fan-out (the former public-brief-ready append was retired by issue #74 — the public brief left the personal-brief scope), the guest edition's default-list sync (issue #213, below), and (issue #56) the **first** completion of interest onboarding with at least one active followed tag, which rebuilds today's edition in place — from `PUT /api/reader/interests` (兴趣页「完成」) or `POST /api/reader/interests/onboarding/complete` (早报横幅「稍后再说」), both sharing one conditional-UPDATE transition and returning `brief_rebuild_status` / `brief_rebuilt`. Rebuild requests are durable, latest-wins desired state. Repeated requests while pending merge into the current edition without extending its readiness deadline; requests during generation record a successor intent that is materialized after the active revision reaches a terminal state, including after restart. Readiness exposes source-sync and analysis completion separately, and a partial edition marks `sync_stale` / `analysis_incomplete` independently from content-selection degradation.

## 游客早报:共享一版(issue #213)

全体游客读同一份按默认订阅编排的个人早报:版面归保留账号 `guest:shared`(早报路由的 `_username` 把访客身份换成它),08:30 定时遍历启用账号时自然包含;当天首位游客打开时 `ensure` 现场生成。游客不能重编(白名单不含);当天版面失败时游客打开会触发一次系统重试(10 分钟内不重复,原因 `manual_rebuild`),默认名单变化时系统以 `subscription_changed` 重编当日游客版。页面对游客:眉头下恒为「游客版 · 按默认订阅编排 · 登录后按你的订阅与兴趣编排」,不出「重新编排」「立即重编」「重试生成」与兴趣引导;选篇理由里的「你订阅的」显示为「默认订阅的」(`frontend/src/utils/guestCopy.js`,只改显示、快照不动)。

Private interest recommendations require a current semantic verdict with `scope=main`: the article must center on the interest as a core argument or key event (2026-11-25). A related section, example, background passage or title phrase alone is not a core match. Old-policy results and frozen editions remain auditable; changing the prompt does not authorize repeating already billed work. Semantic centrality remains a model judgment requiring real-sample evaluation.

### 管理员逐条 AI 新概念审核（issue #298）

默认关闭的 `[taxonomy] review_ai_enabled` 允许管理员对待审核提案发起持久任务。公开证据与目录绑定后调用辅助模型，置信度严格 >0.8 且明确 new、无风险时沿原审批事务创建目录草稿；正式目录仍不自动发布。回写复核提案修订、当前公开证据／正文版本、目录修订与治理权限，重复请求和中断不重发。见 [部署开关](../ops/taxonomy-governance.md)。
