## Article analysis and governed taxonomy (v3.44)

Article persistence is the reliability boundary; analysis is queued afterward and processed by restart-safe leases in `services/article_analysis.py`. A succeeded current asset contains the news-value score (column `quality_score`, semantics since v3.48 = 这件事有多重要, one ruler for reader/personal brief/public brief)/summary/genre plus ordered canonical assignments and up to six extracted display tags. Canonical recall uses names, aliases and `prompt_description`; extracted tags may remain flexible display/search chips without becoming long-term interests. `services/taxonomy.py` owns global taxonomy versions, aliases, parent relations, Candidate evidence/decisions, publication and retag jobs. The approved v1 catalog is repository-controlled; neither bootstrap nor automatic activation may silently publish product vocabulary.

## Personal brief selection (v3.44)

User interests are `follow` only since v3.56 (issue #27 retired the `mute` stance; the column stays for compatibility); all three canonical facets are initially selectable, while taxonomy policy may later cap hot choices per facet. A personal edition freezes the user's subscribed source IDs and interest revision. Interest-matched items occupy at most half the target, score fills the remainder (hard floor 5.0 since v3.48), and an empty subscription scope stays empty. Edition items are immutable snapshots. Since v3.48 editions generate immediately from available content (去等待): `sync_stale`/`analysis_incomplete` are recorded as facts at generation time, not waited on. The public daily brief remains a separate product that reuses the same analysis score. **v3.50 adds a third lane `breaking`(「重大事件」,issue #33 §2)** that deliberately ignores the subscription scope: an event qualifies mechanically when an official source scores ≥ T(default 9.0)or ≥2 distinct sources share an `entity.*` tag with scores ≥ T−0.5 and one ≥ T; events are merged by entity-code connected components, one representative each(official > non-social > score > earlier), at most N(default 2,0 = off)per edition, published within 24h, hidden/`user_rss_` sources excluded, entities featured in the reader's previous two dates suppressed. Breaking items sit at positions 0..N−1 in section「重大事件」**on top of** the target 10 and never count toward the interest ratio; an empty subscription scope still yields no edition. **v3.52.1(issue #33 §4)** localizes item titles after generation: `personal_digest_titles.localize_edition_titles` fills `snapshot.title_zh` from the article's `translation_zh_title` cache, then recent public-brief `title_cn`, then a budgeted aux-model batch translation written back to the cache; Chinese titles are skipped, failures fall back to the original title, the edition never fails because of it. Knobs: KV `personal_digest_breaking_min_score`/`personal_digest_breaking_max_items` via `/api/admin/analysis/config`. See `docs/archive/personal-brief-breaking-lane-plan.md`. **v3.54.0(issue #33 §3)「订阅 ∪ 兴趣」**: the interest half is no longer cut by the subscription boundary——its pool is 订阅源 ∪(全站可见源 ∩ 命中兴趣标签)[v3.56 起无屏蔽]; outside-subscription candidates(`DigestArticleCandidateDTO.subscribed=False`)enter **only** the interest lane, must clear `personal_digest_external_min_score`(default 6.0,订阅内仍 5.0)and share a hard `personal_digest_external_per_source_max`(default 2,never relaxed); subscribed hits rank first; the quality lane stays subscription-only; an interest hit(both pools)means primary tag or relevance ≥ 0.8(same ruler as the reader interest lens); an empty subscription **with** followed interests now yields an interest-only edition(≤ 5 items, ratio lifted), only 订阅+兴趣 both empty stays `empty_subscriptions`; freeze/readiness(`expected_source_ids`/`due`/`sync_stale`)remain subscription-only; feed/MCP unchanged. Editions persist `selection_stats_json`(candidate counts/window/followed/hits/breaking)for the page's「编排说明行」. See `docs/archive/personal-brief-interest-union.md`.

## Daily Brief (每日 AI 资讯日报) — reuse the article analysis score, then deterministic render

`src/services/daily_brief.py` orchestrates a scheduled/manual digest of already-archived articles. Since v3.48 (issue #22) the brief **does not score articles itself**: the platform has one scoring ruler — the article-analysis news-value prompt (`llm/article_analysis_prompt.py`) — and the brief consumes it. Pipeline: `collect_candidates` (cursor/allowlist/batch caps; overflow persists as pending in `daily_brief_candidates`; default batches reserve up to 40 backlog slots then admit new items within 120 total / 60 per source) → `load_stored_scores` (batch-read `article_analyses` rows that are `succeeded` **and** carry the current `scoring_version`; older rulers count as absent) → `score_candidates` (stored scores project directly; candidates the worker has not reached are scored **in place by the same `analyze_article_with_llm`** fed the same `load_relevant_active_tags` closed set the worker uses (so realm/company/tags share one vocabulary), concurrent + one serial retry; body-less candidates are title-scored by the same ruler; results are used for this run only and **never written back** — the brief and the analysis worker stay decoupled, so the brief works with the analysis switch off or with zero analysed rows, worst-case cost = the old map) → threshold (`daily_brief_min_score` KV, default 6.0, 0 = off: below-threshold items are passed — neither selected nor appended, skipped by the cursor; body-less items that clear it go to the appendix) → `dedup_clusters` (`precluster_same_event` first merges same-company titles that are near-identical after normalisation with matching digit runs — mechanical, LLM-free; then one LLM call clusters the rest; official representative preferred within a 1.0 gap, media links folded into `extra_sources`; LLM failure keeps the mechanical merge) → `select_top` (effective score = score + 0.5×official bounded bonus [v3.35], per-source/per-realm caps and a `paper_cap`; classification/realm/company are derived mechanically from the analysis genre / first topic tag / first entity; preselects top_n+buffer) → `editorial_polish` (**only the preselected dozen** get one LLM call each for title_cn / 1–3 bold key points / 100–150 字点评 / source name / company / realm / tags via `EDITORIAL_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, the retired MAP contract minus score and classification; the analysis summary and `score_reason` are passed in as known facts so the 6000-char body clip never loses the global picture; failure keeps the analysis summary as key points and an empty comment, never demotes to the appendix) → `cross_day_dedup` (small aux-tier JSON call against recent briefs' titles + first key point [excluding the report date]; official items never silently dropped — downgraded to a「（接前报）」followup) → trim to top_n → **same-day merge** (v3.35: rerun merges old∪new instead of overwriting) → `render_brief_markdown` (deterministic code-side rendering, v3.34) → idempotent write of a `daily_brief` content record (`source_id=dorami_daily_brief`). Items whose score never arrived (inline scoring failed after retry) and body-less items that cleared the threshold land in the「📎 其它收录」appendix as title+link — the appendix is no longer an ungated back door. `extensions.items` keeps its historical shape (title_cn/summary/comment/classification/score/tags/company/realm…; `score_reason` is never projected, so it can never be mistaken for the public comment) — the shendeng export and intranet pipeline are unaffected. **Three-layer dedup**: ① the deterministic high-water cursor `daily_brief_cursor` (over `fetched_date`) that only advances after a successful write; ② same-day `dedup_clusters`; ③ `cross_day_dedup`. Brief content, candidate consumption, cursor and last-run statistics commit atomically; generation has a per-database process lock and rejects concurrent scope/cursor changes. A changed explicit cursor resets the ledger for intentional replay; deleting the latest brief preserves old pending rows. Empty-cursor bootstrap is limited to the latest batch, including a fence for historical IDs sharing the watermark timestamp. Run state/config (cron, top-N, min-score, last run [incl. `scored_stored`/`scored_inline`/`scored_inline_pending`/`below_threshold`/`score_histogram`], cursor, **source-scope allowlist** `daily_brief_source_ids`) lives in `AppSettingRecord` KV; the node-local `daily_brief_candidates` ledger retains pending/processed article IDs (CASCADE on article deletion). `candidates_deferred` reports overflow. See `docs/archive/news-coverage-reliability-plan.md`. The allowlist is hand-maintained (unset = all sources). The scheduler registers exactly one APScheduler job `daily_brief`; `reload_daily_brief_schedule()` hot-adds/removes it. **Manual generation (`POST /api/daily-brief/generate`) is a persistent background job** returning `{status: "accepted", job_id}`; the frontend polls `GET /api/jobs/{job_id}`; fine-grained stage progress (collecting/scoring/selecting/editing/reducing/persisting) remains in-memory via `GET /api/daily-brief/progress`. Daily-brief endpoints are **collector(admin)-gated**; the panel (`DailyBriefPanel`) lives in the admin-only「AI 日报」tab.

## Analysis visibility and worker draining (v3.46)

Missing historical analysis is absence, not score zero: reader surfaces omit score/tags until a result exists. Pending/running items show 「正在分析」; a forced same-content refresh keeps the prior result readable and shows 「更新中」 until an authority/content-hash-fenced commit replaces it. Changed content hides stale analysis. One scheduler cycle drains a bounded backlog with controlled concurrency instead of processing only one page, and pending/running metrics are reported separately.

## Personal brief rebuild intent (v3.46; entry points narrowed v3.51.1)

Since v3.51.1(issue #33 §5)reader interest and subscription edits **no longer trigger a same-day rebuild** — they are recorded only, and today's edition surfaces `interest_stale` / `scope_stale`(computed by `personal_digest.edition_freshness` on the today endpoints only)so the page can offer「下次编排生效 · 立即重编」. Rebuilds now come from the reader's manual rebuild, the 08:30 schedule, the public-brief-ready append, the admin source-takedown fan-out, and (issue #56, the one explicit exception) the **first** completion of interest onboarding with at least one active followed tag, which rebuilds today's edition in place — from `PUT /api/reader/interests` (兴趣页「完成」) or `POST /api/reader/interests/onboarding/complete` (早报横幅「稍后再说」), both sharing one conditional-UPDATE transition and returning `brief_rebuild_status` / `brief_rebuilt`; `INTEREST_CHANGED`/`SUBSCRIPTION_CHANGED` stay in the enum for history and the service contract. See `docs/archive/personal-brief-rebuild-entrypoints.md`. Rebuild requests are durable, latest-wins desired state. Repeated requests while pending merge into the current edition without extending its readiness deadline; requests during generation record a successor intent that is materialized after the active revision reaches a terminal state, including after restart. Readiness exposes source-sync and analysis completion separately, and a partial edition marks `sync_stale` / `analysis_incomplete` independently from content-selection degradation.
