There's a short story by Asimov called "The Last Question", in which humanity keeps asking a succession of ever-more-powerful computers how to reverse entropy. Each time the computer says there is insufficient data for a meaningful answer.

I think about this story a lot when people talk about AI alignment as if we were waiting for a machine god to adjudicate our disagreements.
