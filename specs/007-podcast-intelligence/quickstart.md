## 5. Golden Evaluation Assets

P1 starts by creating a reviewable, license-safe fixture set:

- At least 10 feeds: AI core, technology core, mixed, off-topic, insufficient sample and malformed/security cases.
- At least 100 sampled episode metadata records for relevance calibration.
- At least 20 long-episode transcript fixtures for value/summary testing; synthetic or explicitly permitted content only.
- At least 5 TTS scripts covering Chinese names, English model names, numbers, abbreviations and code-switching.

Fixtures record the expected label, rationale and evidence. Human expected labels are never generated by the same prompt under test.

## 6. P1 Acceptance — Source Admission

### API and data

1. Preview an AI-native feed and verify `approved` proposal with sampled evidence.
2. Preview a mixed technology/business feed and verify `review_required` or manual mixed policy.
3. Preview a non-technology feed and verify `rejected_scope`.
4. Preview a feed with fewer than five useful entries and verify `review_required`.
5. Preview private-address, redirect-to-private, oversized, invalid XML and no-enclosure feeds and verify `blocked`.
6. Confirm preview produces zero enclosure audio requests.
7. Import the same signed preview twice and confirm only one source/profile exists.
8. Confirm internal trust tier does not change an off-topic proposal into approved.
9. Confirm manual override requires a reason, records actor/version and expires correctly.
10. Confirm all manual, batch and scheduled fetch entry points refuse non-approved sources.

### Browser

1. Log in as admin and open Podcast source add/preview.
2. Verify sample labels, ratios, confidence, warnings and decision controls are readable.
3. Verify pending/rejected sources never appear in the reader's Podcast discovery catalog.
4. Verify the Podcast add view contains no article/dynamic/social source and no cross-shape collection.
5. Capture desktop and mobile screenshots for the handoff.

### Release gate

- Off-scope false-admission rate under 5% on the gold set.
- Technical/security fixture block rate 100%.
- Preview audio download count 0.
- Migration drift test passes.

## 7. P2 Acceptance — Domain, Rights, Summary and Translation

1. Backfill an existing Podcast episode twice and confirm normalized identity is stable.
2. Verify legacy `/api/articles` Podcast projection remains unchanged/additive.
3. Parse publisher transcript, chapters and license fixtures with documented size and format limits.
4. Verify official transcript path causes zero ASR provider calls.
5. Verify transcript pagination preserves ordered segment IDs and timestamp jumps within two seconds.
6. Verify `link_only` hides derivative generation and publication controls.
7. Verify an episode-level deny overrides source-level permission.
8. Verify original/Chinese title and description switch with AI translation disclosure.
9. Verify relevance and value are displayed independently with metadata/transcript basis and confidence.
10. Verify desktop and mobile show the same core fields and state.

Release gate:

- Original audio start success >=95% across accepted fixtures.
- Publisher transcript timestamp navigation error <=2 seconds.
- Translation never replaces or destroys the source text.
- No rights-less derivative action is exposed.

## 8. P3 Acceptance — ASR and Chinese Digest

1. Verify `duration_seconds=1800` performs zero ASR; `1801` continues to the remaining gates.
2. Verify source, rights, relevance, preliminary value, budget and resource failures stop before ASR.
3. Force a retryable provider failure and verify lease/retry recovery without duplicate artifacts or cost rows.
4. Submit empty, repeating, wrong-language and invalid-timestamp transcripts and verify QA rejection.
5. Verify final value score is transcript-based and retains all dimensions/version/evidence.
6. Attempt to publish a digest with a missing/fabricated segment ID and verify rejection.
7. Compare names, organizations, numbers and model names against the gold transcript.
8. Verify every published important claim has one or more valid source segments/time spans.
9. Re-run with a new prompt/model revision and verify the old artifact is superseded, not overwritten.
10. Verify publish, unpublish and takedown are audited and user visibility updates immediately.

Release gate:

- `Precision@K >=0.8` for high-value candidates.
- Claim evidence coverage >=95%.
- Named entity and number consistency >=98%.
- Human “publishable without major rewrite” rate >=85%.
- Recoverable pipeline success >=95%.

## 9. P4 Acceptance — <=15-Minute Chinese Audio

1. Verify only a reviewed narration script with audio derivative rights can enter TTS.
2. Verify the configured voice is a licensed platform voice and not a source speaker clone.
3. Measure all golden audio using ffprobe; every published artifact must be <=900 seconds.
4. Verify 901–960 seconds triggers script reduction and resynthesis.
5. Verify >960 seconds triggers script regeneration; after two failed attempts it enters human review.
6. Verify no large playback-speed adjustment is used to pass the limit.
7. Verify silence, truncation, loudness and media-container checks block bad output.
8. Verify original/digest variants use separate labels, URLs, ranges and playback progress.
9. Revoke rights and confirm new access fails immediately and existing served URL expires within the configured SLA.
10. Verify AI disclosure, original attribution and evidence navigation on desktop and mobile.

Release gate:

- 100% published digest audio <=900 seconds; median 12–14 minutes.
- No cloned voice or misleading source-author attribution.
- Median complete processing cost <=$0.35 per 60-minute source; >$0.60 requires explicit approval.

## 10. Final Main-Session Checklist

The main session performs and records:

- Branch range and unrelated-diff audit against the chosen target branch.
- Alembic upgrade from a copy of a representative legacy database and a fresh database.
- Full targeted Podcast test suite, migration drift, route permission and reader-shape tests.
- Frontend lint/build and desktop/mobile browser E2E on isolated ports.
- Real RSS smoke against a small approved set; no production database mutation.
- Provider smoke only with explicit configured credentials and budget; logs are checked for secret leakage.
- Rights-deny, budget-deny, retry, input-change and takedown failure paths.
- Artifact hashes, screenshots, audio sample and ffprobe duration report.
- Documentation/contract/task status update and final product-decision confirmation.

No agent-reported “tests passed” replaces this main-session acceptance.
